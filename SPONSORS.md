# Sponsors · 赞助支持

**BountyRadar MCP** is released under the **MIT license** — free to use, fork, and
build on. There are **no strings attached** to the hosted endpoint; you only pay
Apify's Pay-Per-Event rate when you call the live tools.

## Why support us?

BountyRadar is one of a portfolio of open, auditable MCP servers from PanStories.
Each one is built to be **agent-first, deterministic, and human-verified** — no
affiliate junk, no black-box ranking. Maintaining the aggregation pipeline,
source adapters, and scoring logic takes real engineering time.

If BountyRadar saves you hours of bounty-board scrolling, a small recurring
tip keeps the feed fresh and the servers running.
